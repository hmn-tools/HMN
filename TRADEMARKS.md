# The name and the mark

The source code of HMN is under the Apache License, Version 2.0. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The licence does not give a right to the name or to the mark. Section 6 of the licence says this for names and marks in general. This file says it for HMN.

- The name is "HMN".
- The mark is the figure in the files under `server/assets/logo/`, and the same figure in the two apps.

What you can do without a request:

- Say that your software works with HMN, or that it is made from HMN.
- Keep the name and the mark in an unchanged copy of this repository.

What needs the agreement of the HMN authors:

- The name or the mark as the name, the icon, or the logo of a changed version, a hosted service, or another product.
- A use that tells people that the HMN authors made or approved your product.

If you publish a changed version, give it another name and another icon.
