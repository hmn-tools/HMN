Your whole life in one quiet place.

Your tasks are in one app, your calendar is in another, and the school dates are in a chat. No agent can help with a life that it cannot see. HMN puts it all in one place on your Mac, and your agent works across all of it, inside privacy limits that you set.

Agents

Agents propose. You decide.

Here an agent read a note and saw that the car insurance ends this month. It prepared the task and gave its reason. Nothing changes until you approve, and you can undo it.

Calm

A screen that you can leave.

Today shows what needs you, and then it ends. There is nothing below it to scroll.

Yours

It lives at home, on your own Mac.

There is no account and no subscription. Change your agent or your phone, and your life stays where it is. When the Mac sleeps, the phone tells you.
